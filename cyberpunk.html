Cyberpunk 2077

Action RPG Sci-Fi / Cyberpunk CD PROJEKT RED Update 2.0 / Phantom Liberty

📖 Description

Cyberpunk 2077 is an open-world, action-adventure RPG set in Night City, a neon-soaked megalopolis obsessed with power, glamour, and relentless body modification. Players embody V, a customizable mercenary outlaw in pursuit of a prototype neural chip containing the digitized psyche of rock rebel Johnny Silverhand.

🗡️ Story

After a high-stakes heist at Arasaka Corporation turns catastrophic, V is left fighting against time as the biochip slowly overwrites their consciousness. Navigating between corrupt megacorps, ruthless street gangs, and enigmatic fixers, V must make decisions that determine not only their survival, but the very soul of Night City.

⭐ Verdict & Review

Our Score: ★★★★☆ 4.0 / 5.0 (Great Experience)

With the monumental 2.0 update and the Phantom Liberty spy-thriller expansion, Cyberpunk 2077 fulfilled its immense promise. Featuring redesigned cyberware, vehicle combat, smart AI, and some of the best ray-traced visuals in PC gaming history, it delivers an unforgettable cyberpunk thrill ride.

  • OS: 64-bit Windows 10
  • CPU: Intel Core i7-6700 or AMD Ryzen 5 1600
  • RAM: 12 GB
  • GPU: GTX 1060 6GB or Radeon RX 580

Recommended (1080p / 60 FPS Ultra)

  • OS: 64-bit Windows 10 / 11
  • CPU: Intel Core i7-12700 or AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D
  • RAM: 16 GB - 32 GB
  • GPU: RTX 3070 / RTX 4070 or Radeon RX 6800 XT
  • Storage: 70 GB NVMe SSD
