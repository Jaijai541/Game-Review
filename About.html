🎮 Our Mission

Welcome to Cir Pink's Game Review Hub — a passion project built by gamers, for gamers. In an era where game marketing often overshadows actual gameplay, our mission is simple: provide honest, aesthetic, and community-driven reviews that help you discover which games are truly worth your time and hard-earned money.

From expansive AAA open worlds to competitive mobile MOBAs and mesmerizing indie RPGs, we evaluate games based on storytelling, mechanics, visual design, sound design, and player value.

⭐ How We Review (The Rubric)

Every review on Cir Pink's follows a consistent, transparent scoring methodology so you can calibrate your expectations at a glance:

★★★★★ Masterpiece: An unforgettable milestone in gaming with world-class narrative, mechanics, and design.
★★★★☆ Great: Exceptionally enjoyable and polished, recommended wholeheartedly with minimal minor flaws.
★★★☆☆ Decent: Solid foundation and entertaining gameplay, but may feel repetitive or niche.
★★☆☆☆ Mediocre: Suffer from balance issues, optimization bugs, or lackluster mechanics.
★☆☆☆☆ Not Recommended: Severe bugs, predatory monetization, or uninspired design.
