The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

Action-Adventure Open World Nintendo EPD Release: 2023

📖 Description

An epic adventure across the vast land, soaring sky islands, and eerie Depths of Hyrule. Building upon the beloved foundation of Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom introduces mind-bending Ultrahand crafting, Fuse weapon synthesis, and Ascend vertical mobility that redefine emergent sandbox gameplay.

🗡️ Story

Following the re-awakening of an ancient malevolence beneath Hyrule Castle, Princess Zelda vanishes through time while Link awakens with a mysterious Zonai right arm. To rescue Zelda and restore the kingdom from the clutches of the Demon King Ganondorf, Link must uncover the truth behind the Imprisoning War and the legendary Tears of the Dragon.

⭐ Verdict & Review

Our Score: ★★★★★ 5.0 / 5.0 (Essential Masterpiece)

Tears of the Kingdom is a staggering achievement in player agency and physics engine design. The seamless layering of Sky, Surface, and Depths creates hundreds of hours of organic discovery. Even with minor hardware limitations on the Switch, it stands as one of the greatest video games ever crafted.
