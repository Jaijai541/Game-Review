Baldur's Gate 3

CRPG / Tactical Turn-Based Dungeons & Dragons 5e Larian Studios Game of the Year

📖 Description

Baldur's Gate 3 is a monumental, story-rich party-based roleplaying game set in the Forgotten Realms. Faithfully translating the fifth-edition D&D ruleset into video game form, it offers unprecedented narrative reactivity where every dialogue choice, dice roll, and environmental interaction dynamically shapes your adventure.

🗡️ Story

Abducted by Mind Flayers and infected with an illithid tadpole burrowing into your brain, you begin transforming into a monster. But as the parasite grows, it also grants you strange psionic powers. Alongside a richly written cast of morally complex companions, you journey across the Sword Coast to find a cure—only to discover that your condition is tied to an apocalyptic conspiracy threatening all Faerûn.

⭐ Verdict & Review

Our Score: ★★★★★ 5.0 / 5.0 (Essential Masterpiece)

Larian Studios has set a new high-water mark for the RPG genre. With phenomenal voice acting, endlessly creative turn-based combat encounters, and profound choice-and-consequence writing across three sprawling acts, Baldur's Gate 3 is nothing short of legendary.

⚙️ System Requirements (PC)

Minimum Specifications

  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • CPU: Intel Core i5-4690 or AMD FX 8350
  • RAM: 8 GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA GTX 970 or AMD RX 480 (4GB VRAM)
  • Storage: 150 GB free SSD space required

Recommended Specifications

  • OS: Windows 10 / 11 64-bit
  • CPU: Intel Core i7-8700K or AMD Ryzen 5 3600
  • RAM: 16 GB DDR4
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 2060 Super or AMD RX 5700 XT (8GB VRAM)
  • Storage: 150 GB free SSD space required
