Reverse: 1999

Tactical Card RPG Retro-Occult / 20th Century Bluepoch Full English Voice Acting

📖 Description

Reverse: 1999 is a stylish 20th-century time-travel strategic RPG by Bluepoch. Marrying vintage Art Deco aesthetics with British theatre voice acting and occult mysticism, players manipulate arcanum card combinations in turn-based combat while investigating an inverted temporal storm.

🗡️ Story

On the final day of the millennium—December 31, 1999—a supernatural phenomenon known as the "Storm" causes time to reverse, unwinding humanity backwards through eras like 1960s swinging London and the 1920s Roaring Twenties. Players step into the shoes of Vertin, the Timekeeper appointed by the St. Pavlov Foundation, who alone remains immune to temporal erasure as she seeks the truth behind the Storm and the enigmatic Manus Vindictae.

⭐ Verdict & Review

Our Score: ★★★★★ 5.0 / 5.0 (Aesthetic Masterpiece)

Reverse: 1999 is one of the most culturally distinctive gacha RPGs ever released. Its cinematic Live2D art direction, jazz-infused vintage soundtrack, evocative prose, and card synthesis combat system create an irresistible atmosphere unlike anything else on the market.
